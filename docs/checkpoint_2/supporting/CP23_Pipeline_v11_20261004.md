# CP2.3 Pipeline v1.1: Development Coverage

**Date:** 4 October 2026. **Status:** Development experiment complete; the coverage target was not met. Pipeline v1 and its original records remain unchanged.

## Question

Can a versioned pipeline change raise the proportion of the same 60 development CV/JD pairs that receive a final or provisional score, without hiding failed calls or unreviewed requirements? The target is at least 54 of 60 pairs. Original top-K order metrics still require every candidate in the original cutoff to have an eligible score and relevance judgment.

## Diagnosis and changes

The [40-pair diagnosis](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/diagnosis_v1.json) classifies the pairs without a final v1 score: 13 linked to suspected JD output limits, three to JD inventory mapping or schema failure, 12 to structural `needs_review`, two to `looks_incomplete`, four to invalid matching output, two to transport timeout, three to zero scored required units, and one to F00369 source provenance. This is a partition of pairs, not distinct JDs. The old ledger did not save `finish_reason`; 25 failed JD calls and one matching call reached exactly the old 16,000-token allowance. That is strong evidence of truncation, not proof of the old provider finish reason. Invalid final matching outputs did not retain a safe exact validator code, so the historical reason cannot be invented.

Pipeline v1.1 changes only supported causes. It sizes the output allowance from local estimated input tokens and expected units. Successful Part B ledger calls gave p99 output/input ratios of 2.004421 for JD extraction (49 calls), 1.546403 for evidence matching (49), and 9.030508 for CV parsing (three). Each ratio has a 50 percent margin. The formula is `min(model_output_limit, max(floor, ceil(ratio x input_tokens + per_unit_allowance x expected_units)))`. JD extraction uses floor 20,000 and 96 tokens per expected unit; matching uses 18,000 and 64; CV parsing uses 12,000 and 128. Floors exceed the observed old 16,000-token cutoff; per-unit values are additional engineering buffers above the ratio estimate, not independent fitted forecasts. The [calibration receipt](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/output_policy_calibration_v1.json) states the sample sizes and exact values. The conservative output cap is 131,072, the smallest published maximum among the inspected [official OpenRouter DeepSeek V4.1 Flash endpoints](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash/endpoints) on 4 October 2026. Billing uses generated tokens, while the guard reserves the larger allowance before each call. The local ratio evidence is small, especially for CV parsing; the new limit is not a guarantee of complete model output.

The client now records `max_tokens` and `finish_reason`. A response with `finish_reason=length` is `truncated` and gets at most one fresh completion with double the allowance, capped by the model limit. This is separate from the existing one validation repair. Old errors and old model answers stay unchanged. The v1.1 request timeout is 240 seconds versus 90 seconds in v1. Matching has a four-worker limit, a process-wide ledger lock and a local in-flight reservation check for both the run cap and project guard. A timeout is recorded without a hidden retry. Model mapping and semantic failures are still failures; no validator was relaxed.

Under [D-071](../../decisions.md), H2 excludes unresolved logical units from the score denominator only when at most 20 percent of scored required logical units are unresolved. Each excluded unit is recorded with its ID, text, source quote and reason. The score is provisional and requires the UI text "syarat yang perlu dicek". More than 20 percent, or another failure, remains on hold. H1 remains the historical comparison. The [saved-output counterfactual](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/hold_options_v1.json) gave H1 20/60 and H2 30/60 usable scores before any new inference. H2 does not make an incomplete score equivalent to a final score.

## Setup and budget

The [v1.1 plan](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/plan_v1.json) binds the original Part B ranking and protected-input hashes. It reruns 16 failed or held JDs and up to 25 affected pairs, while reusing valid v1 results. The original scope is two synthetic development CVs, 52 distinct development JDs and 60 ranked pairs. The model is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, with JD prompt v1.4, evidence prompt v1.1, guideline v1.3, quote validator v1.1 and G1/G2. The [versioned configuration](../../../config/versions/pipeline_cp23_v11_20261004.yaml) does not replace historical or active runtime configuration.

During the technical audit, F00310 returned zero units despite an inline "Qualifications and Experience:" section and 68 reviewed A units. The old source-inventory pattern missed this heading. A new source-based guard rejects an empty output for it without reading gold at runtime. F00309 was initially suspected of the same defect, but its saved extraction contains nine units, all preferred; the current source inventory also recognizes its standalone "Preferred competencies and qualifications" heading. The bounded follow-up therefore targeted F00310 and the two jobs that exceeded the approved H2 threshold, F00022 and F00126. It used the same model, prompt, source scope, ledger run ID and US$3.00 aggregate cap. No result was selected merely because it agreed with gold.

The median cost per successful historical call, multiplied by 1.5 across planned stages, estimates **US$0.373196173**. The separately approved v1.1 cap is **US$3.00**. The numeric local project settings before dispatch were budget 19.0 and hard stop 18.5; the ledger was US$2.4590349434. The larger `max_tokens` raises each guard reservation even when actual billed output is shorter. The run checks aggregate cost and in-flight upper bounds before each request. No key or CV text is logged.

The first dispatch was interrupted while its provider response was still streaming. It produced no saved JD result. The append-only ledger retains **US$0.0501633** as an uncertain upper-bound reservation, not a confirmed provider charge. That historical row has `ok=true` because `KeyboardInterrupt` bypassed the older `except Exception` handler; its `cost_source=uncertain_upper_bound` and absent finish reason identify it as unresolved. The handler now records interruptions as failed with the reservation intact, and a regression test covers this case. The same JD was then retried once under this new v1.1 plan; both attempts count against the US$3.00 cap. No historical ledger line was deleted or rewritten.

## Bounded follow-up and remaining causes

The primary v1.1 run saved all 60 pair records. A separate [follow-up plan](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/hold_continuation_plan_v2.json) used the same US$3.00 aggregate cap for three JDs and four pairs. F00022 still has 4 of 15 required units marked `needs_review`, above 20 percent. F00126 still has 1 of 4, also above 20 percent. Matching completed for both, but H2 correctly retained `on_hold`. F00310's previously empty answer was rejected by the new source-section guard; its follow-up output then failed exact JD quote validation. Both associated pairs remain held. No validator rule was loosened.

F00309 was removed from the paid follow-up during preflight. The saved extraction already contains nine units, all preferred under the source heading. It has no required-score denominator. Repeating its extraction to obtain a different answer would not be justified by the documented empty-output bug. Its `no_score` status is retained. F00351 and F00559 also lack assessable required units in the available source. F00369 is a separate, unresolved source-provenance hold. These cases limit the attainable coverage within this frozen 60-pair sample.

## Order metrics, latency and cost

The [D-052 evaluation](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/evaluation_v2.json) preserves every original candidate position and reports H1 and H2 for K=10, 20 and 30, each with PARTIAL weights 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75. At weight 0.5 under H2, CV1 has 7/10, 14/20 and 20/30 scored candidates; CV2 has 9/10, 14/20 and 22/30. **Zero of 36 final-order metric cells are complete.** Missing scores or relevance judgments are never set to zero or replaced by lower-ranked jobs. The original stage-1 P@5 values are 0.2 for CV1 and 0.4 for CV2; final P@5 and NDCG@10 remain unavailable. H4, K and the PARTIAL weight therefore remain provisional.

The primary run's observed wall time was 2,648 seconds for a resumed mixed workload covering both CV lists, including shared JD work and reused stage outputs. The follow-up has separate stage timings but no clean one-CV K20 end-to-end timing. D-068's latency condition is **not measured** by this experiment. Four-worker matching was implemented and exercised, but this does not establish a production service latency.

The [ledger-backed coverage receipt](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/coverage_summary_v2.json) records **US$0.6281001646** against the US$3.00 aggregate cap across 57 v1.1 calls. That total includes **US$0.0501633** of uncertain reservation from an interrupted first dispatch, not a confirmed provider charge. The project ledger accounts for **US$3.087135** after the run. This experiment made no calls on test data and did not write gold or source files.

The [semantic spot check](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/semantic_spot_v1.json) found an unsupported communication MATCH based on a thesis-title quote, an Office-suite requirement merged across three reviewed units, and a qualifier mismatch in a prompt-engineering reference. The sample is not a full semantic audit. Exact source quotes and a numeric score do not prove that a requirement is correctly interpreted or that CV evidence entails the claim.

The final [technical source audit](../../../evals/results/cp23/pipeline_v11/technical_qa_v2.json) checked 60 pair identities, 1,099 JD quote occurrences and 695 CV quote occurrences after the continuation. It found no exact-source or identity fault. It did not resolve the three semantic findings above.

## Final verification

The full local test suite passed with **474 passed and 2 skipped** on 4 October 2026. Three historical Stage-2 plan tests initially failed because the new preferred-heading inventory changed a frozen cost estimate. The historical planner now calls its versioned v1 inventory, while pipeline v1.1 retains the stronger current source guard. The targeted historical and v1.1 tests passed before the full suite. All **20 protected input hashes** in the final coverage receipt still match, including the workbook, gold, source corpus, split, prompts and synthetic CVs. No test inference or gold write occurred. The skipped tests were not counted as passes.

## Decisions and limits

D-070 revises the budget ceiling and D-071 approves H2 for this version. The v1 score formula, gold, source JDs and CVs, workbook, split and pool are unchanged. H2 changes the denominator only for identified unresolved units, so the UI and evaluation must disclose the exclusions. A technically valid output still needs semantic quality review. The historical 16,000-token failures cannot be reclassified with certainty because their provider finish reasons were not saved. The [coverage chart](../../../reports/figures/cp2/fig08_pipeline_v11_coverage_20261004_v3.png) shows the before/after counts. This development experiment is complete, while CP2.3 remains open because the coverage target, H4/K/weight evaluation, D-050 broad extraction and D-051 privacy gate are unresolved. The held-out CP2.4 test must remain untouched until the D-053 freeze gate.
