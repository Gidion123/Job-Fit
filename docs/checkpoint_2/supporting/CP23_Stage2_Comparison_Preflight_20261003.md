### Findings and interpretation

The number of emitted units differs by candidate, so raw requirement counts cannot silently determine comparable match percentages.

5. **Latency and cost are measurable, quality selection is not yet resolved.** Gemini was fastest in this sequential observation, but had one process failure. GPT incurred lower extraction cost than the other candidates, but its sampling parameter differs from the others and its semantic checks did not all pass. Timings include allowed validation/repair, not source QA, key checks, full CV parsing/matching or user-response latency. This was not a randomized or repeated production benchmark.
